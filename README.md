# Homestead vs. Non-Homestead: Unequal Exposure to Tax Foreclosure in Kalamazoo County

Gular Abbasli, December 2025

## About

This paper studies the determinants of tax foreclosure in Kalamazoo County using administrative data from the County Treasurer’s Office. The main focus of the analysis is on property level predictors of foreclosure. In particular, I study whether non-homestead parcels face higher foreclosure risk than owner occupied parcels, and how financial stress indicators such as unpaid utilities, large forfeited balances, and repeated delinquency shape outcomes.

By combining descriptive statistics with linear probability and logistic regression models, this paper aims to provide a clearer picture of which parcels are most vulnerable to foreclosure.
